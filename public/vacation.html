My Favorite Vacation Spot: Russia

If I could pick one place to travel to, it would be Russia.

Why Russia?

It's not a place a lot of people I know have been to, and that's exactly what draws me to it. It seems like a genuinely cool country to experience — a mix of history, architecture, and culture that's totally different from anywhere I've traveled before. Going somewhere that most people haven't been is what makes a trip feel like a real adventure instead of just another vacation.

Why it's on my list

Uncommon destination
Not many people I know have visited.
Different experience
A culture and history unlike anywhere I've been.
Sense of adventure
Off the beaten path compared to typical vacation spots.
